## Clock correction (do not use the first-pass Gold / EST story)

The first `results.json` treated broker walls as UTC or as `Europe/Athens`. That inverted Gold and invented an “EST naive is still large (premarket)” story. Those claims are withdrawn.

**Transform (every FP / Vantage series admitted to this lane):** drop the file’s tz label, subtract 7 hours, localize `America/New_York`, convert to UTC. Server wall = ET+7 (EET/EEST on **US** DST dates). A single `server_utc_offset_sec=10800` is an Aug-2026 EEST snapshot and is not applied.

**Admission test (hard refuse, not warn):** resample both books to hourly last close, take log returns, shift the candidate −4..+4h, require the correlation **argmax to be exactly lag 0**. Same-asset floor is 0.95 (true lag is 0.99+). Cross-asset floors are **per pair**. A series with no pairing and no UTC-native yardstick flag is refused — Oil / a longer NQ file cannot land unchecked. `catalog_and_load()` drops failures before the event study / scalp matrix. Fixtures cover EDT, EST, a US-on/EU-off shoulder, C1 (lag −3 refuse), and lag-0 pass. Documented unit: Vantage `2025-03-05 15:00` server → `13:00` UTC (Dukas). Tests: `tests/test_ny_1330_clocks.py` (no live 5y I/O).

ETH / QQQ / DXY now pass the guard (see `results.json` `clock_validation`). Oil is still missing.

**Ambiguous DST:** `server_wall_to_utc` uses `ambiguous="NaT"` and drops those bars (attrs `ambiguous_dropped`). Safer for research than `infer` (raises) or a fold guess. ### 5.1 Is 13:30 a real structural move?

**Hypothesis verdict:** “There are always big moves at 13:30 UTC” is **false as stated**. What is true on the files we have:

1. **US cash open (09:30 ET)** is a high-vol window on **Nasdaq/Dow CFDs** (~3.5–3.9× typical 30m; top-3 on ~9/10 M5 days). It is loud in **both** EDT and EST.
2. That window is **13:30 UTC only during EDT**. In EST it is **14:30 UTC**. Naive 13:30 UTC in EST is 08:30 ET and is **quiet** (~0.23% US100, ~0.14% US30) — not a large premarket.
3. **Gold’s local M15 book *does* spike at cash open** (0.36% vs first-pass 0.15%; 2.18× same-day median). Loud hours are **13–15 UTC**, not 16–18. Still no costed trade edge. Naive 13:30 UTC in EST is 0.00311 against a cash open of 0.00350 (89%); on US100 the same comparison is 34%. Gold’s elevation is a broad 13–15 UTC plateau, not a sharp open spike, so the winter wrong-clock penalty barely applies.
4. **FX and BTC** do not give a clean, unique 13:30 event.
